Institutional Disconnect

While electricity grid access reaches nearly 98.5% of barangays, telecommunications infrastructure severely lags behind at 36.3%. This creates a paradox: communities have electricity to power devices, but no signal to connect to global digital networks.

Geographic Isolation (GIDA): High Cost per Tower

Infrastructure Sharing & Open Access

Policy & Legislative Reform

Mandate common tower sharing and open-access broadband rules (Open Access in Data Transmission Act) to lower CAPEX for telcos in remote areas.

Target: DICT, NTC, Congress CAPEX Reduction: ~40%
#3

National Free Public Wi-Fi Expansion

RA 10929 Scaling

Accelerate the implementation of Free Wi-Fi in Public Places in rural schools, municipal halls, and rural health units with dedicated LGU maintenance funding.

Focus: Public Schools & Health Units Status: Scaling Phase
#4

Satellite Internet Backhaul (LEO)

Island & GIDA Connection

Leverage Low Earth Orbit (LEO) satellite technology to provide rapid backhaul internet to isolated islands where undersea fiber optic cables are cost-prohibitive.

Tech: Starlink / LEO Constellations Deployment: Immediate

Human Capital Capacity

Solution 1 Case Study

Solar Digital Community Centers (DCCs)

DOI: 10.3389/frma.2025.1488916

Based on empirical research by Ortiz Osejo et al. (2025) published in Frontiers in Research Metrics and Analytics, evaluating the UN Energy Compact implementation.

Empirical Guatemala Case

The UN Energy Compact project (executed with New Sun Road) deployed solar microgrid centers in remote indigenous communities, establishing key benchmarks:

  • 100% Clean Power: Off-grid solar + storage units eliminating fossil generator reliance.
  • Local Language Instruction: Curriculum delivered in Q’eqchi’ and K’iche’, achieving high participation.
  • Gender Dynamics: Integration of "Positive Masculinity Workshops" to engage local male leaders and reduce cultural opposition to female digital literacy.

Philippine GIDA Adaptation

Adapting the model to Philippine island geography and cultural contexts:

  • Language Customization: Training modules translated into Tagalog, Cebuano, Ilocano, and Hiligaynon.
  • Travel Reduction: Reduces administrative travel time to municipal halls by up to 75% by hosting digital LGU services locally.
  • Typhoon Hardening: Solar panels with modular collapsible mounts designed for Philippine disaster resilience.

Measured Outcomes

Digital Inclusion Increase +300% Female participation rate in digital literacy
Cost Savings per Household ₱1,200 / mo Saved on fuel, charging fees, & transit

SMART Indicators Framework

Monitoring & evaluation metrics to ensure accountability and long-term viability.

S

Specific

Establish 500 Solar Digital Community Centers in target GIDA barangays across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao.

M

Measurable

Increase barangay cellular/broadband access from 36.3% to 65% and achieve 80% completion in local digital literacy programs.

A

Achievable

Funded through joint DICT Universal Access Fund, LGU counterpart funds, and international climate-tech grants.

R

Relevant

Directly aligns with the Philippine Development Plan (PDP) 2023-2028 and Sustainable Development Goals (SDG 7 & SDG 9).

T

Time-Bound

Phase 1 Pilot (Months 1-12), Phase 2 Scaling (Months 13-36), Phase 3 Institutionalization & Full LGU Transfer (Months 37-60).
